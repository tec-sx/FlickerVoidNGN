# Cameras

- Cameras are directed, never player-controlled (owner, 2026-11-22; `Docs/Plan.md`): the `Cameras` module (over Rendering and Flow; `game.AddCameras()`) has shots, camera entities with a `shot` component (`Shot`: `ShotKey`s with position, rotation, zoom (orthographic height or field of view, by the camera's projection; 0 keeps the camera's), time, `ShotEase` and marker tag, in the entity's space; a `ShotDrive`; whether the scene starts on it; a target, the entity named or else the first `cameraTarget` (`CameraTarget`: the height cameras aim at), which it may look at instead of taking the keys' rotations; smoothing), whose keys make a rail (`Rail` in Runtime's `Geometry`: a centripetal Catmull-Rom spline through every point, measured by arc length, with the nearest point) and a timeline (`ShotTimeline`: along the rail by arc length, slerped and zoomed between keys, eased by the later key). `CameraDirector` (a frame system and service) keeps one shot live (the earliest `start` shot, else the earliest shot), switches with a `ShotTransition` (`Switch`, `Push` and `Return` over a stack; `TransitionKind` `Cut`, `CrossFade` (the old shot keeps moving and its live view dissolves away), `Fade` (to a colour over the duration, cut while dark, back in over the in duration; `FadeOut` and `FadeIn` alone too; the world only, never the interface) or `Blend` (from the drawn view to the next shot, eased, so rails chain); `IsFading`, `IsTransitioning`), plays a timed shot to a key or the end (`Play`, forwards or back; `ShotFinishedEvent`), moves a tracked shot to its target's nearest point on the rail, smoothed, setting its time in proportion (a corridor shot), sends `ShotMarkerEvent` for each marked key passed either way and `ShotChangedEvent`, and writes the live shot's posed lens and world pose into its `ActiveCamera` (Rendering), which `WorldExtractor` draws first, with the transition it lays over the frame (`RenderScene.Transition`), leaving the shot's transform as authored; per-shot driving and posing is `ShotDriver`'s. Camera layers (`Cameras/Layers`) add small, bounded offsets in the camera's own space (`CameraOffset`: move, turn, zoom fraction) on top of the rail without owning the camera: a shot names a `cameraRig` data asset (`CameraRig`: polymorphic `CameraLayer`s, registered with `RegisterDerived<CameraLayer, T>`, each a `CameraLayer<TState>` whose state the director keeps per shot; `CameraLayerRunner`), `follow` (leans after the target within a radius), `look` (drifts towards `CameraDirector.Pointer`, which the game sets, outside a deadzone and settles back), `shake` (smooth noise within its amplitudes, started by `CameraShakeCommand` and fading over its duration, or constant) and `zoomPunch` (`ZoomPunchCommand`); a shot whose rig lacks a shake or zoom punch gets a default one, so commands always show, and `Play(shot, key, layers: false)` turns the layers off until it gets there, for cutscenes. Triggers (`Cameras/Triggers`): physics has no trigger volumes, so a `triggerArea` (`TriggerArea`: a box size, an offset in the entity's space, required tags the activator must own) is checked each fixed step by `TriggerAreaSystem` (a service) against `triggerActivator`s, sending `AreaEnteredEvent` and `AreaExitedEvent`; generic, so music or story can use them too. A `cameraCue` on an area (`CameraCue`: shot by entity name, `ShotSwitchMode` `Switch`, `Push` or `Return`, transition, on `Enter`, `Exit` or `Both` (one area that goes back on the way out: to its `ExitShot` or the shot live on entering, or returning when it pushed, with its `ExitTransition`, only when it fired on entering), play to a key with or without layers, shake, once, and `When`, fact conditions, since components can't hold polymorphic conditions) becomes a director request (`CameraCueSystem`; saves keep which saveable cues fired). Scripting (`Cameras/Scripting`): the effects `cutToShot`, `playShot` and `shakeCamera`, so interaction offers use cameras without Cameras knowing Interaction, and the flow nodes `switchShot` (waits for its transition), `playShot` (waits for the shot to finish), `waitForShotMarker`, `fade` (out or in) and `shakeCamera`, which wait through the director's `WhenTransitionEnds`, `WhenShotFinishes` and `WhenMarker` and cancel in `Cleanup` (`FlowWaiter`); a cutscene is a flow graph that pushes a shot, plays it without layers and returns. A `skippable` shot may be skipped while it plays (`CanSkip`, `Skip`: it jumps to where it plays to, sending the markers it passes and `ShotFinishedEvent`). Saves keep the director as game-wide state (`cameras`, `SavedCameras` by shot names: the live shot, the stack, each shot's time and play, the fade); a load cuts to the saved shot with no transition running. `TransitionPass` (Cameras, `Shaders/Transition.hlsl`), which `AddCameras` adds after `OutlinePass` to every renderer the host creates through Rendering's `SceneRendererSetup` service, renders the dissolving view with a `SceneRenderer` of its own and blends it and the fade colour over the output; whatever reads the view (`PointerPicker`, the walk control, the minimap) reads `ActiveCamera.Find`, the active camera's pose or else the first camera's. The depth fade (owner, 2026-11-23: artistic control, not realism, like a painter darkening what is near) darkens what stands nearer than a focus and melts what lies farther into a colour, each ramping between a start and an end distance along the view up to its amount: the scene's main camera (Rendering's `mainCamera`, `MainCamera`) holds it for every camera, a `depthFadeOverride` (`DepthFadeOverride`) replaces its near or far side for one camera, and the director writes the live shot's fade (`MainCamera.FindFor`), measured from the shot's target and blended through blends and cross-fades, into `ActiveCamera` (`DepthFade`, `Focus`); without a director the first camera's fade is drawn, measured from the camera. `DepthFadePass` (Rendering, `DepthFade.hlsl`) lays it on the lit scene before the upscale and the grade, from the scene's depth (`FrameData`), only where there is paint and never on the sky: what is nearer darkens as if a shadow fell on it, and the far colour, picked for the screen, is turned back through the mood's grade, so the far side takes it on screen; `SceneRenderer.DepthFades`, `DepthFade` and `Focus` switch or replace it, and the editor's viewport draws the main camera's fade (its preview the selected camera's) measured from the first camera target, with a toggle in its bar.
- Cutaways (`Cameras/Cutaways`, owner 2026-11-23): `CutawaySystem` (a frame system and service) makes the smallest indoor volume around what the cameras follow (`CameraDirector.FindTarget`: the live shot's target, else the first `cameraTarget` spawned) the `Cutaway`, and marks `ShadowOnly` everything nested under the same top entity (the building, whose storeys are nested indoor volumes) standing at or above that volume's ceiling: the floors above and the roof. Outside every volume nothing is cut. A world editor is planned for later.
- Trigger areas count what stands inside them again when a save loads, without sending events, so a load fires no cues.
