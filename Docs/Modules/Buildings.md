# Buildings

- Buildings (the `Buildings` module over Creations, Physics and Rendering, milestone 5 of the creation tools; `game.AddBuildings()`) are made in the editor's building creator, opened by a double-click on a placed building (in place) or on a building file in the asset browser (alone). New, Building writes a ground floor with an entrance under a roof. A building is a creation (`ICreationFile`, `Creations.md`): the mesh tools, mirror and the door tool work on its parts as they do on a vehicle's.
- A building file (`Name.fvbuilding`, `BuildingFile`, schema `building`) holds `size` (the width, the height from one floor to the next and the depth new storeys are made with; 8 by 3 by 6), `wall` and `floor` thicknesses, `storeys` from the bottom up (`BuildingStorey`: `level`, -1 for a cellar, 0 for the ground floor; the `shell` part; `ambient`), a `roof`, `stairs`, an `elevator` (`BuildingElevator`: the `platform` part and `speed`) and `doors`. Part ids: `cellar`, `storey.0`, `storey.1`, `roof`, `stairs.1`, `elevator`, `door.1`. A file whose levels don't follow one another from a cellar or the ground floor can't be read.
- Shapes (`BuildingShapes`): a storey's shell is a tray, a floor with four walls and no ceiling (the storey above or the roof closes it); stairs are a column per step, so every face stays convex, with steps no higher than `StepRise` (0.15 m: lower than the paths' climb with room for the navigation cells' rounding); the roof and the elevator's platform are slabs.
- `SetStoreys(above, cellar)` adds storeys on top and takes them off the top (1 to `MaxStoreys`, 12), adds or removes the cellar, and moves the roof; the storeys that stay keep their shape. `AddStairs(level, foot, run)` stands a flight on the level's floor climbing toward +Z to the next floor. `SetElevator(place)` rests a platform on the lowest floor; `GetElevatorStops` gives how far above its rest it stands at each storey.
- Placed in a level, a building is an entity with the `building` component (`Building`: the `file`). `BuildingSpawnSystem` gives Creations the building kind: parts spawn nested under the entity in the editor and the game, each drawn by a `MeshRenderer` named `<file>#<part id>` (`BuildingMeshSource`). Storeys, roof and stairs are `StaticBody`s whose `TriangleCollider` is the part's own triangles, so paths walk their floors, steps and openings as they are (`ObstacleGeometry`, `Characters.md`). Each storey also carries an `IndoorVolume` of its inside (the cellar mutes the outside), so the `CutawaySystem` opens the storey the camera's target stands in and hides what is above it; it finds a part's building through `NestedUnder`. Doors spawn with their joint and toggle like a vehicle's, and are opened by a click (`PartOffers`). A change to the file respawns the parts, keeping those whose ids stay.
- The elevator: its platform is a kinematic hull on a slider joint with a `Toggle`, an `Elevator` (the stops, the speed, the stop it is at) and a `UsePoint` with `StandOn`, so whoever uses it walks onto it first. `ElevatorSystem` hears each `ToggledEvent` on it and sends it to the next stop up, and from the top back to the bottom; characters standing on it ride along. It cannot be called from another floor yet, and a save keeps the toggle but not the exact stop.
- The creator's tools (`BuildingTools`, Editor Core `Buildings`, as they need Booleans): `SetStoreys` also drops flights of stairs that lost a floor and keeps the elevator on the lowest floor with its shaft cut through new floors; `AddStairs` adds a flight and opens the floor above over it, ending the opening where the top step does so the step meets the floor; `AddElevator` cuts the shaft through every floor above the lowest; `AddEntrance` opens a doorway (1.2 by 2.1 m) in the middle of the ground floor's front wall, toward -Z. New flights start one metre from the back wall, in one of two lanes by level so a flight's foot stands clear of the opening below it (`GetStairsFoot`); the elevator stands against the right wall (`GetElevatorPlace`). Without the Booleans' native library the parts are added and nothing is cut.
- The creator's panel (`BuildingPanelViewModel` over `BuildingDocument`, a `CreationDocument<BuildingFile>`; `BuildingPanelView`): Storeys (1 to 12), Cellar, Stairs (the storey to climb from, and Add) and Elevator. Each choice is one undo step in the file's history, held unsaved until saved, and reaches every placed copy. `BuildingWalkTests` (Editor Core) place a building the tools made and walk a character in through the entrance and up the stairs.
- Not there yet: looks from Blender for buildings, windows, moving or removing a flight from the panel (undo, or the mesh tools, do it), calling the elevator.
