# Buildings

- Buildings (the `Buildings` module over Creations, Physics, Rendering and Cameras, milestone 5 of the creation tools; `game.AddBuildings()`) are drawn as plans in the editor's building creator, opened by a double-click on a placed building (in place) or on a building file in the asset browser (alone). New, Building writes a ground floor of one room (the Hall) with a front door under a flat roof. A building is a creation (`ICreationFile`, `Creations.md`): the mesh tools, mirror and the door tool work on its parts as they do on a vehicle's, and a part reshaped by hand is kept until its plan changes.
- A building plays before it has looks (owner, 2026-11-27): doors open and lock, stairs climb, elevators are called and ridden and shots switch from the plan alone; models from Blender later replace only the looks.

## The file

- A building file (`Name.fvbuilding`, `BuildingFile`, schema `building`) holds `size` (the width, storey height and depth new storeys are made with; 8 by 3 by 6), `wall` and `floor` thicknesses, `storeys` from the bottom up, a `roof`, `stairs`, `elevators`, `shots` and `doors` hung by the door tool. A file whose levels don't follow one another from a cellar (-1) or the ground floor (0) can't be read. Files written before plans keep their storeys' `shell` part and their roof's solid.
- A storey (`BuildingStorey`: `level`, `height`, `wall`, `floor`, `ambient`) is a plan: `corners` (ids and points), `walls` between two corners (`PlanWall`: `outside`, an optional thickness, a height of full, half or custom, and a `bulge` that bends it into an arc of `segments` pieces), `rooms` (`PlanRoom`: a name and a point inside the walls that close it, with its own ambient), `pillars` (`PlanPillar`: a point, a size, square or round), `cornerPillars` (one at every corner where outside walls turn, `cornerPillarSize`) and `openings` (`PlanOpening`: a `Door`, `Doorway` or `Window` on a wall at a distance along it, or a `Floor` hole at a point; width, height, lift, a flat or arched top; a door's leaf (thickness, hinge side, outward, angle, glass) and its lock (`locked`, the `key` tag, `consumeKey`, `pickable`, `difficulty`)). Ids are shared by everything in a storey (`NextId`).
- Stairs (`BuildingStairs`: the level climbed from, the foot's middle, a heading in degrees from +Z toward +X, `Straight`, `L` or `U`, width, first run and landing, rise, tread, `meetsFloor` so the floor above is the top step, margin, headroom, railing). The floor above is opened wherever a climber's head would meet it, and the opening ends where the stairs arrive, so the floor meets the top step without a gap (`StairsLayout`).
- An elevator (`BuildingElevator`: the shaft's middle, size, heading (the side it is stepped onto), `from` and `to` levels, speed, call buttons, railing) cuts its shaft through every floor above its lowest stop, a hairline (`ShaftClearance`, 0.02 m) wider than its platform so walkers step across. A call button stands beside the shaft on each stop's floor (`GetCallButton`).
- The roof (`BuildingRoof`: `Flat`, `Shed` or `Gable`, rise, overhang, thickness, along the depth, or none).
- A shot (`BuildingShot`: name, level, place and turn, orthographic size, follow and look) has `triggers` (`ShotTrigger`: walking into a room, through an opening, onto stairs or onto an elevator; on entering, leaving or both; a cut, blend or fade over a time; on leaving both, back to the room's shot or the one live before).
- `BuildingMaker.Make` makes every part from the plans (Booleans cut openings and holes when the editor's native library is there): part ids `storey.N.floor`, `.walls` (the outside walls), `.wall.{id}` (inside walls), `.door.{id}` (a leaf on its hinge), `.frame.{id}`, `.glass.{id}`, `.pillar.{id}`, `.corner.{id}`, `stairs.{id}` and `.railing`, `elevator.{id}`, `.railing` and `.call.{level}`, `shot.{id}` and `roof` (a cellar's prefix is `cellar`).

## In the game

- Placed in a level, a building is an entity with the `building` component (`Building`: the `file`). `BuildingSpawnSystem` gives Creations the building kind: parts spawn nested under the entity in the editor and the game, each drawn by a `MeshRenderer` named `<file>#<part id>` (`BuildingMeshSource`). Floors, walls, pillars and stairs are static bodies whose triangle colliders paths walk as they are (`ObstacleGeometry`). Each storey carries an `IndoorVolume` of its inside and each room its own (`BuildingRegions`), so the `CutawaySystem` opens the storey the camera's target stands in and hides what is above it.
- Doors spawn on their joint with a toggle and a `UsePoint` at their middle; `PartOffers` offers them to interactors, so a click opens them (`Interaction.md`). A locked opening adds a `Lockable` with its key tag, so the game's key and lockpick rules apply. Doors are path obstacles; platforms are not, so paths keep the floor under them.
- The elevator's platform is a kinematic hull on a slider joint with a `Toggle`, an `Elevator` (stops, levels, the stop it is at) and a `UsePoint` with `StandOn`. Riding (`ElevatorSystem`) takes it a stop on the way it goes, turning at the top and bottom; a call button (`ElevatorCall`) brings it to its stop. Whoever clicks it walks onto it (`MoveCommand` with `Onto`, as paths stop at the shaft) and rides along.
- Each shot spawns as a `PlacedShot` with its lens and rig, and each trigger as an area (`BuildingCue`); `BuildingCueSystem` switches the camera director to the shot as the trigger says.

## The creator

- The building creator (`BuildingEditor`, an `ICreatorTool` in Editor Core, over `BuildingTools` and `PlanEdits`) works on one storey at a time from the plan tools on the viewport's rail: Select (Q, with the gizmo's Move, Rotate and Scale), Push wall (drag a wall along its normal; neighbours stretch and `Carry openings` keeps openings on them in place), Move corner, Split wall, Arc, Wall (drawn between points, joining and splitting what it meets), Room (a rectangle of inside walls), Pillar, Opening (D: a door, doorway or window on a wall, a hole in the floor), Stairs (S), Elevator (T), Roof (O) and Camera (Y: drops a shot looking at the room clicked, with a room trigger). Every drag is one undo step, pending until Apply (Enter) or Cancel (Escape) on the confirm bar; P toggles a top view, and edits snap to the grid step (0.1 m by default).
- The Tool tab (`BuildingToolViewModel`) shows the tool's settings, or what is picked (a wall's length, thickness, height and what follows it; a corner; a room's name and area; an opening's size, leaf and lock; a pillar; stairs; an elevator; a shot with its triggers and Look through), each change one undo step; while an edit waits, the walls and rooms that follow it show what they were and what they become (`BuildingEditor.Before`). Picking stairs or a shot in the tree goes to its storey. The Building tab (`BuildingPanelViewModel`) lists the storeys top first with Above, Cellar, Copy and Remove, and the parts tree (storeys, floors, walls, rooms, openings, pillars, stairs, elevators, shots and the roof), which picks the part clicked. The viewport's storey switcher picks the storey; the storeys above it, with the railings round openings in their floors, are hidden unless its eye is on. Labels name the rooms with their areas, and tags measure drags.
- Walk (the breadcrumb's toggle; `BuildingWalk`) plays the building in a world of its own: ground, the building, a walker outside the first door, a shot outside looking over the walker at the building, and the building's shots, drawn through the live shot, which the walk bar names. Clicks pass through what the cutaway hides; a door, call button or platform clicked is walked to and used. Escape or Walk again stops it.
- `BuildingWalkTests` and `BuildingWalkSessionTests` (Editor Core) walk in through doors, up stairs, call and ride elevators from any stop and watch shots switch; Bleeders' `CoachingInnTests` play a building made with these tools by clicks.
