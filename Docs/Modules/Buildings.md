# Buildings

- Buildings (the `Buildings` module over Creations, Physics, Rendering and Cameras, milestone 5 of the creation tools; `game.AddBuildings()`) are drawn as plans in the editor's building creator, opened by a double-click on a placed building (in place) or on a building file in the asset browser (alone). New, Building writes a ground floor of one room (the Hall) with a front door under a flat roof. A building is a creation (`ICreationFile`, `Creations.md`): the mesh tools, mirror and the door tool work on its parts as they do on a vehicle's, and a part reshaped by hand is kept until its plan changes.
- A building plays before it has looks (owner, 2026-11-27): doors open and lock, stairs climb, elevators are called and ridden and shots switch from the plan alone; models from Blender later replace only the looks.

## The file

- A building file (`Name.fvbuilding`, `BuildingFile`, schema `building`) holds `size` (the width, storey height and depth new storeys are made with; 8 by 3 by 6), `wall` and `floor` thicknesses, `storeys` from the bottom up, a `roof`, `stairs`, `elevators`, `shots` and `doors` hung by the door tool. A file whose levels don't follow one another from a cellar (-1) or the ground floor (0) can't be read. Files written before plans keep their storeys' `shell` part and their roof's solid.
- A storey (`BuildingStorey`: `level`, `height`, `wall`, `floor`, `ambient`) is a plan: `corners` (ids and points), `walls` between two corners (`PlanWall`: `outside`, an optional thickness, a height of full, half or custom, and a `bulge` that bends it into an arc of `segments` pieces), `rooms` (`PlanRoom`: a name and a point inside the walls that close it, with its own ambient), `pillars` (`PlanPillar`: a point, a size, square or round), `cornerPillars` (one at every corner where outside walls turn, `cornerPillarSize`) and `openings` (`PlanOpening`: a `Door`, `Doorway` or `Window` on a wall at a distance along it, or a `Floor` hole at a point; width, height, lift, a flat or arched top; a `frame` round it, a window's `glass` pane to see through; a door's leaf (thickness, hinge side, outward, angle, `double` for two leaves meeting in the middle, a window in each leaf by `windowWidth`, `windowHeight` and `windowFrom` the floor, with `glass` in it), its handle (`handleHeight`, `handleInset` from the free edge: where a hand takes the leaf, kept for the hand's IK later) and its lock (`locked`, the `key` tag, `consumeKey`, `pickable`, `difficulty`)). Ids are shared by everything in a storey (`NextId`).
- Stairs (`BuildingStairs`: the level climbed from, the foot's middle, a heading in degrees from +Z toward +X, `Straight`, `L` or `U`, width, first run and landing, rise, tread, `meetsFloor` so the floor above is the top step, margin, headroom, railing). The floor above is opened wherever a climber's head would meet it, and the opening ends where the stairs arrive, so the floor meets the top step without a gap (`StairsLayout`).
- An elevator (`BuildingElevator`: the shaft's middle, size, heading (the side it is stepped onto), `from` and `to` levels, speed, call buttons, railing) cuts its shaft through every floor above its lowest stop, a hairline (`ShaftClearance`, 0.02 m) wider than its platform so walkers step across. A call button stands beside the shaft on each stop's floor (`GetCallButton`).
- The roof (`BuildingRoof`: `Flat`, `Shed` or `Gable`, rise, overhang, thickness, along the depth, or none).
- A shot (`BuildingShot`: name, level, place and turn, orthographic size, follow and look) has `triggers` (`ShotTrigger`: walking into a room, through an opening, onto stairs or onto an elevator; on entering, leaving or both; a cut, blend or fade over a time; on leaving both, back to the room's shot or the one live before).
- `BuildingMaker.Make` makes every part from the plans (Booleans cut openings and holes when the editor's native library is there): part ids `storey.N.floor`, `.walls` (the outside walls), `.wall.{id}` (inside walls), `.door.{id}` (a leaf on its hinge, with `.b` for a double door's second leaf and `.glass` for the pane in a leaf's window), `.frame.{id}`, `.glass.{id}`, `.pillar.{id}`, `.corner.{id}`, `stairs.{id}` and `.railing`, `elevator.{id}`, `.railing` and `.call.{level}`, `shot.{id}` and `roof` (a cellar's prefix is `cellar`).

## In the game

- Placed in a level, a building is an entity with the `building` component (`Building`: the `file`). `BuildingSpawnSystem` gives Creations the building kind: parts spawn nested under the entity in the editor and the game, each drawn by a `MeshRenderer` named `<file>#<part id>` (`BuildingMeshSource`; floors in a darker grey than the rest, so a storey reads from above, where a wall's top and the floor face the same way). Floors, walls, pillars and stairs are static bodies whose triangle colliders paths walk as they are (`ObstacleGeometry`). Each storey carries an `IndoorVolume` of its inside and each room its own (`BuildingRegions`), so the `CutawaySystem` opens the storey the camera's target stands in and hides what is above it.
- Doors spawn on their joint with a toggle and a `UsePoint` at their middle; `PartOffers` offers them to interactors, so a click opens them (`Interaction.md`). A locked opening adds a `Lockable` with its key tag, so the game's key and lockpick rules apply. Doors are path obstacles; platforms are not, so paths keep the floor under them.
- The elevator's platform is a kinematic hull on a slider joint with a `Toggle`, an `Elevator` (stops, levels, the stop it is at) and a `UsePoint` with `StandOn`. Riding (`ElevatorSystem`) takes it a stop on the way it goes, turning at the top and bottom; a call button (`ElevatorCall`) brings it to its stop. Whoever clicks it walks onto it (`MoveCommand` with `Onto`, as paths stop at the shaft) and rides along.
- Each shot spawns as a `PlacedShot` with its lens and rig, and each trigger as an area (`BuildingCue`); `BuildingCueSystem` switches the camera director to the shot as the trigger says.

## The creator

- The building creator (`BuildingEditor`, an `ICreatorTool` in Editor Core, over `BuildingTools`, `PlanEdits` and `PlanPicker`) works on one storey at a time from the plan tools on the viewport's rail: Select (Q), Push wall, Move corner, Split wall, Arc, Wall (drawn between points, joining and splitting what it meets; its ends snap to walls and corners, wall middles and the grid, each a switch in the Tool tab), Room (a rectangle of inside walls), Pillar, Opening (D: a door, doorway or window on a wall, a hole in the floor), Stairs (S), Elevator (T), Roof (O) and Camera (Y: drops a shot looking at the room clicked, with a room trigger). Every drag is one undo step, pending until Apply (Enter) or Cancel (Escape) on the confirm bar; P toggles a top view, and edits snap to the grid step (0.1 m by default). Only one rail tool is lit: the level's Move, Rotate and Scale are the plan's gizmo (`BuildingEditor.Gizmo`, `GizmoMode`), which takes the Select tool, and a plan tool puts the gizmo away.
- Picking (owner, 2026-11-28) is a ray against what stands on the storey (`PlanPicker.Pick`: wall faces at any height, arcs by their pieces, openings by their cut, pillars, steps, elevator squares, rooms by their floor; the nearest hit wins, and a tool only takes what it works on, `Takes`), with handles hit in screen space within 12 pixels (`GrabPixels`: corners at their foot or head, a wall's push arrow, an arc's middle, an opening's slide and lift arrows, a door's handle, the stairs' and elevator's squares, shots). A drag moves on the level plane at the height it was grabbed at, so a wall grabbed high up moves by what the mouse moved, and a corner taken by its head stays under the pointer (`PressCorner` keeps where it was taken, `_grabOffset`). Taking a tool keeps what is picked only when the tool works on it (`Keeps`), nothing behind the gizmo is hovered while the pointer is on it, and nothing stays hovered once the pointer leaves the viewport (`Leave`).
- The gizmo works on what is picked in the plan: it slides a wall, a corner, a pillar, an opening along its wall, a door's handle over its leaf, stairs, an elevator or a shot, turns stairs, an elevator or a shot (in steps of `TurnStep` while snap is on), and sizes a pillar, an opening, stairs, an elevator or a shot's view; its edit waits for Apply like any other.
- The plan's look is the renderer's flat look (`Rendering.md`) with overlays in the mockups' colours: what is picked is filled and outlined in the accent's orange (`PickFill`, bold lines), what the pointer is over faintly (`HoverFill`, thin lines), what an edit cuts is striped red (`CutFill`, hatched), glass is blue, and what plays (hinges, swings, lift arrows, headings) is green. The dashed ghost of what is being edited stays where it stood while its edit waits, tagged "where it was".
- A picked door shows how it plays: each leaf's hinge as a green line with its two hinges, the way its free edge swings as an arc on the floor with the leaf standing open at its end, its window in blue, and its handle as an orange ball that is picked by a click and dragged over the leaf (`PlanPickKind.Handle`; ringed white while picked). Tags name the hinge with the way it opens and the handle with its height and inset. The Opening tool adds the double arrow that slides it along its wall, the green arrow that lifts it, and every other opening's name and lift. An opening that reaches past its wall or its floor, or whose cut runs into another opening's (`OpeningShape.FindOverlapped`, `BuildingEditor.GetMisfit`), is drawn red, says why in its tag and its Cut section, and a toast says so when it is let go.
- The storeys below and above the one edited are hidden with their stairs, call buttons and shots, unless the storey switcher's eye is on (`BuildingPanelViewModel.ShowsOthers`, `BuildingPlanViewModel.IsHiddenPart`); the railing round an opening that stairs come up through stays. An elevator passing the storey is marked by its square on the floor and its shaft through the storey in dashes. The roof is hidden but while it is picked in Parts or its tool is in hand with nothing else picked (`BuildingEditor.ShowsWhole`), when the whole building stands under it and nothing of one storey is drawn through its walls.
- The Stairs and Elevator tools open the storey (`ShowsOpenStorey`): only its floor is drawn, its walls as their lines on it, the floor above see-through over it on thin lines at its corners, striped red wherever stairs, shafts and holes cut it, with a tag naming the cut; every elevator stands as a dashed shaft from its lowest stop to its highest with a tag of its stops, and picked stairs show their steps filled, handles for their width and runs, and tags for their foot and their steps. A shaft's railings are hidden meanwhile.
- What a tool refuses it says in a toast (`Warned`, rule R26): stairs on the top storey ("add a storey above first"), an opening on no wall, a wall or room dragged too short, an opening that doesn't fit where it was dropped.
- The Tool tab (`BuildingToolViewModel`) shows the tool's settings, or what is picked: a wall (length, thickness, height, outside and what follows it; with the Arc tool its bulge, segments, side, chord and length along the arc); a corner; a room's name and area; an opening in sections (Opening, Place, Size, Leaf with one or two leaves and the lock, Handle, Frame and window, Cut); a pillar; stairs (Stairs, Steps, Opening above, Camera); an elevator; the roof; a shot with its triggers and Look through, and with the Camera tool the building's shots with a warning on stairs that have none. Each change is one undo step; while an edit waits, the walls and rooms that follow it show what they were and what they become (`BuildingEditor.Before`). Sections that stay take new values in place, so a box that holds the keyboard is never rebuilt under it. Picking stairs or a shot in the tree goes to its storey. The Building tab (`BuildingPanelViewModel`) lists the storeys top first with Above, Cellar, Copy and Remove, and the parts tree (storeys, floors, walls, rooms, openings, pillars, stairs, elevators, shots and the roof), which picks the part clicked.
- Labels name the rooms with their areas (their shots with the Camera tool), and tags measure what is picked and dragged (`GetLabels`, `PlanLabel` with an offset on screen so a tag sits beside its handle). Tags that would cover each other or a room's name step apart, the tag of the drag in hand keeping its place (`PlanTagLayout`).
- Walk (the breadcrumb's toggle; `BuildingWalk`) plays the building in a world of its own: ground, the building, a walker outside the first door, a shot outside looking over the walker at the building, and the building's shots, drawn through the live shot, which the walk bar names. Clicks pass through what the cutaway hides; a door, call button or platform clicked is walked to and used. Escape or Walk again stops it.
- `BuildingEditorTests`, `PlanPickerTests`, `PlanTagLayoutTests` and `BuildingToolViewModelTests` drive the tools by presses and drags through the camera; `BuildingWalkTests` and `BuildingWalkSessionTests` (Editor Core) walk in through doors, up stairs, call and ride elevators from any stop and watch shots switch; Bleeders' `CoachingInnTests` play a building made with these tools by clicks.
