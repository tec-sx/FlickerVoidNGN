# Props

- Props (the `Props` module over Creations, Physics and Rendering; `game.AddProps()`, owner 2026-11-27) are objects with no rules of their own: a crate, a room, a car to look at, a simple open box. They are made in the editor's prop creator, opened by a double-click on a placed prop (in place) or on a prop file in the asset browser (alone). New, Prop writes one metre box standing on its origin. A prop is a creation (`ICreationFile`, `Creations.md`): the mesh tools, mirror and the door tool work on its parts as they do on a vehicle's, so an open box is the box hollowed (H) with its top face selected.
- A prop file (`Name.fvprop`, `PropFile`, schema `prop`) holds `parts` (`PropPart`: a `name`, its id, and the `part`, a `SolidPart`) and `doors`. Part ids: the parts' names (`part.1`, `part.2`), then `door.1`, `door.2`. `AddBox(size, at)` adds a box named with the first free number; `Remove(name)` takes a part out with the doors placed on it.
- Placed in a level, a prop is an entity with the `prop` component (`Prop`: the `file`). `PropSpawnSystem` gives Creations the prop kind: parts spawn nested under the entity in the editor and the game, each drawn by a `MeshRenderer` named `<file>#<part id>` (`PropMeshSource`) and solid as a `StaticBody` of its own triangles; doors swing on their joints as kinematic hulls with their toggle, opened by a click. A change to the file respawns the parts, keeping those whose ids stay.
- The creator's panel (`PropPanelViewModel` over `PropDocument`, a `CreationDocument<PropFile>`; `PropPanelView`): the size of the next box and Add box (it stands on the ground half a metre to the right of the parts already there), and a part picked and Remove (the last part stays). Each is one undo step in the file's history, held unsaved until saved, and reaches every placed copy.
- Not there yet: parts other than boxes from the panel (the mesh tools shape them), moving a whole part (its points move with the mesh gizmo), looks from Blender, and moving or rigid props.
