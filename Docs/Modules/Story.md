# Story and dialogue

- Story (the `Story` module over Flow, ported from FVStorySystem; `game.AddStory()`) is quests and knowledge kept in facts, so saves hold them. A quest (`QuestDefinition`, type `quest`) keeps its `QuestState` in the fact its id names: it starts by `AutoStartWhen` or a rule, fails by `FailWhen`, completes when its required objectives are done (each `QuestObjective` sets its fact to 1 when visible and `CompleteWhen` passes; a quest with none is completed only by a rule), applies `OnStarted`, `OnCompleted` or `OnFailed`, and runs its `.fvflow` while active. `QuestSystem` (a fixed system and service) follows every quest of the content folder (`DataAssets.LoadAll`), evaluates them on each fact change (re-entrant, at most `MaxPasses`), starts with the game's first step and sends `QuestChangedEvent`. Knowledge (`KnowledgeDefinition`, type `knowledge`: a `KnowledgeKind`, prerequisite ids and `OnLearned`) is learned through `KnowledgeSystem` (a service), which sets its fact to 1. Rules name quests and knowledge by id: the `questState` and `knows` conditions read the facts, the `setQuestState` and `learn` effects go through the services, and the flow nodes `setQuestState`, `completeObjective` and `waitForQuestState` drive quests from graphs.
- Dialogue (the `Dialogue` module over Flow, ported from FVDialogueSystem; `game.AddDialogue()`) runs one conversation at a time on flow graphs. A `dialogueParticipant` (`Speaker`, the character id lines name; `Dialogue`, its `.fvflow`) is someone to talk to; `DialogueSystem` (a fixed system and service) starts the owner's graph with the instigator as the run's instigator and the owner as its target (`Start`, or the `startConversation` effect a Talk offer applies), and talks to the interface through events (`ConversationStartedEvent`, `LineStartedEvent`, `LineFinishedEvent`, `ChoicesPresentedEvent`, `ConversationEndedEvent`, `BarkEvent`), which answers with `Advance`, `Choose` and `End` (also the player's way to leave). The `line` node shows a line (speaker resolved to an entity, duration from `DialogueSettings` when it has none, or waiting for input), the `choice` node offers at most three options (`First`, `Second`, `Third`), hidden or shown locked by their conditions' failure presentation, and the `bark` node says a fitting row of a `bark` table (identity, required and blocking tags, conditions), not the speaker's last. The conversation ends when its graph finishes or has nothing left to do, when it is ended, or when its owner is gone. There is no audio yet, so lines carry no voice.
- Quest flows are saved (`questFlows`, by quest, the active node ids) and resume where they were when a save loads, and a new `Player` moves every running flow to it. Dialogue ends the conversation when a save loads or its scene is left.
