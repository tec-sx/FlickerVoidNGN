# Creations

- Creations (the `Creations` module over Runtime, milestone 1 of the creation tools) spawn the parts of things made in the editor's creators, such as a vehicle's body and wheels. A kind of creation describes each part as `PartData`: an id unique in the creation (`body`, `wheel.1L`), its place in the space of the entity placed in the level (its pivot) and the components the kind gives it (`With<T>`, such as its mesh). `CreationParts` (one per world, over the game's `EntityLifecycle`) takes a kind with `AddKind<T>(describe)`: when an entity with the kind's component attaches, in the editor and the game, one entity per part is spawned and started, marked `CreationPart` (its root and id) and nested under the root with `TransformHierarchy.Attach` (Runtime's `NestedUnder`, a parent given as the entity, since parts have ids and not scene names), so it follows the root; when the root detaches, its parts are destroyed, last first. `Patch(root, parts)` brings them in line with a newer description by id, as scenes are patched by entity name: new ids are spawned, gone ones destroyed, and kept parts stay the same entities, so a selection survives an edit, with only what changed since the last patch set: the place, and each component between its stop and start hooks. Parts that share an id are refused before anything changes. `Find` and `FindAll` give a root's parts. Parts are not scene entities, so a save of the level (`ISceneSpawner.Capture`) writes none of them, and without `Saveable` game saves keep none either.
- Every part carries its rest place as `PartPlace`. Joints are kinematic (owner, 2026-11-25): no physics constraints, as a swing or slide is easily posed. A kind gives a part a `PartJoint` (`With`), a point with no mesh in the root's space: a `Hinge` turns the part about `Axis` through `Pivot` by degrees, a `Slider` moves it along `Axis` by a distance, between `Min` and `Max`, at `Speed` a second (at once when not above zero). As the part attaches it gets a `JointPose` (`Position`, `Target`) at its rest place, or its nearest limit, and is posed there; gameplay moves it by setting `Target` on the part (`Find(root, id)`). `JointSystem` (a frame system `AddCreations` adds) moves each position toward its clamped target, nothing while paused, and places the part under its root with `TransformHierarchy.Attach` at `JointSystem.Pose(rest, joint, position)`, so it still follows the root. Joints are given in code for now: creation files and the creators place them with the door tool (milestone 2).
- A door is a part with a joint and a toggle (milestone 2, `Vehicles.md` for the file): its `Toggle` (Interaction) says whether it is open. `JointSystem` starts a part with an open toggle at the joint's open position (`PartJoint.GetOpenPosition()`: the limit farther from rest, so a door hinged the other way has a negative one), and follows `ToggledEvent`s on a part: open sends the target to the open position, shut back to rest. `Snap(part, open)` puts a joint there at once. A save keeps which toggle parts are open for each saveable root (`PartToggleSaves`, `SavedPartToggles`: part id to open, key `partToggles`; the parts themselves are never saved) and a load restores them with `Snap`; a part the creation no longer has is skipped. `Creations` now references `Interaction` for this, and `AddCreations` adds the `JointSystem`, its saves and the `CreationsJsonContext` data resolver.
- A creation's file holds its parts as `SolidPart` (the place of its pivot, its closed `SolidMesh`, the creator's mirror switch, and for a door its `joint` and `toggle`), and shows itself to the creators' shared tools as an `ICreationFile` (its parts by id and its `Doors`). While the game plays, a part with a toggle and no interactable of its own is offered to interactors by `PartOffers` (a service `AddCreations` adds): one offer of the toggle's action on `Input` (`Input.Use` unless the game sets another), so a door the door tool placed opens on a click. Kinds give such a part a `UsePoint` (Interaction) at the middle of its mesh (`SolidPart.GetMiddle`, the grab point for a placed door), since a door cut from a body keeps the body's pivot. A placed door has a `DoorFit` (`fit`: its wall part, place, normal and right, size, cut depths, hinge side, direction and angle, frame, window and grab point, in the wall's space) and its wall part keeps the uncut solid as `source`; the editor makes the wall's `mesh` and the door's mesh and joint from them (`Editor.md`, door tool), so the game only reads the finished parts.
