# Interaction

- Interaction (the `Interaction` module over Platform, ported from FVInteractionSystem for point and click; `game.AddInteraction()`) lets interactors use interactables. An `interactable` component names an `InteractableDefinition` data asset (type `interactable`: up to three `InteractionOffer`s, each an input tag, an action tag, a `Gesture`, display, conditions, effects, weight and uses; a cooldown; the interactor kinds it accepts), loaded from `DataAssets` when the entity activates into an `ActiveInteractable` (state, uses left, cooldown left). An `interactor` (kind, reach) is focused on one with `FocusCommand` (the one under the mouse); `InteractionSystem.GetOffers` lists the focus's offers for prompts; `InteractionInput` presses or releases an input, and a press within reach starts the heaviest offer for it that can be done (offers may share an input: a door's Use unlocks it while locked and opens it after), followed by a `GestureTracker` until it succeeds or ends (`InteractionEnd`). A success sends `InteractionActionEvent`, uses the offer up (every offer used up completes the interactable, else it rests for its cooldown) and applies its effects with the interactor as instigator and the interactable as target. Responses handle the action event: a `lock` (`Lockable`, locked by the action it names, with key and pick settings; the `locked` condition reads it) and a `toggle` (opened and closed by its action, blocked while locked, `ToggledEvent`, `ToggleBlockedEvent`); the `interactorTags` condition checks the interactor's `OwnedTags`. Walking to what was clicked is the game's controller's job, over `Characters` (Bleeders' `ClickControlSystem`). A physics collider's `Offset` places its shape in the entity's space (a door's panel beside its hinge). Conditions and effects read the game's facts, `Game.Facts` (an `IFactDatabase` service).
