# Vehicles

- Vehicles (the `Vehicles` module over Runtime, milestone 1 of the creation tools; `game.AddVehicles()`) are made in the editor's vehicle creator. A `.fvvehicle` (`VehicleFile`, JSON with the `vehicle` schema) holds the `type` (`car` or `motorcycle`), the `body` and the `wheels`, each a `VehiclePart`: its `place` (its pivot, in the vehicle's space) and its `mesh`, a closed `SolidMesh`. Vehicles face +Z with left at +X. Wheels are listed front axle first, left before right, and part ids follow from the order (`GetParts`): `body`, then `wheel.1L` and `wheel.1R`, or `wheel.1` where an axle has one wheel (a motorcycle on two). A car takes 4, 6 or 8 wheels and a motorcycle 2 or 4 (`GetWheelCounts`) until driving (milestone 4) settles the full rules. A file with another count, or with a mesh that isn't closed, can't be read, so the content check reports it. `Create` makes a box body over two axles of cylinder wheels sized for the type. `SetWheelCount` adds axles behind the last or removes the last ones and leaves every other part as it was. `SetType` takes the count nearest the one the vehicle has that the new type takes, and keeps the body, and the wheels while they sit the same way, one or two to an axle.
- A vehicle is placed in a level as an entity with the `vehicle` component (`Vehicle`: the `file`, a `.fvvehicle`). `AddVehicles` (once per game, however often it is called) adds `Creations` (`game.AddCreations()`) and gives it the vehicle kind (`VehicleSpawnSystem`), so its parts spawn nested under the entity in the editor and the game, each drawn with a `MeshRenderer` named `<file>#<part id>` (`Vehicles/Van.fvvehicle#wheel.1L`) that `VehicleMeshSource`, added to the game's `MeshSources`, makes from the file's mesh. Files are read through the game's `IContentManager` service (`SceneHost` and the editor register theirs): without it, as in the content check, a vehicle spawns no parts. When the file changes on disk or the editor writes it, its vehicles are patched by part id (`CreationParts.Patch`), so kept parts stay the same entities, and the meshes are made again. A file that can't be read keeps the parts and meshes of its last good version and logs a warning (`VehicleFiles`); one that never could spawns none. The editor's game adds vehicles before the project's modules, and the template's launcher adds them to its game.
