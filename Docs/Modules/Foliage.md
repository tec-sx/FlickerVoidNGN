# Foliage

- Foliage (the `Foliage` module over Creations, Physics and Rendering, milestone 7 of the creation tools; `game.AddFoliage()`) is made in the editor's foliage creator, opened by a double-click on a placed plant (in place) or on a foliage file in the asset browser (alone). New, Tree writes a tree grown from seed 1. A plant is a creation (`ICreationFile`, `Creations.md`): the mesh tools and mirror work on its parts as they do on a vehicle's; it has no doors, so the door tool hangs none.
- A foliage file (`Name.fvfoliage`, `FoliageFile`, schema `foliage`) holds `kind` (`Tree` or `Bush`), `seed`, `shape` (`FoliageShape`: `height`, `trunkRadius`, `limbs`, `crownRadius`, `crowns`; a tree starts 6 m tall with four limbs and five crowns, a bush 1 m tall with three crowns and no trunk) and the parts growing made: `trunk`, `limbs` and `crowns`. Part ids: `trunk`, `limb.1`, `crown.1`.
- Growing (`FoliageFile.Grow`, also `Create(kind, seed)` and `SetKind`) draws every chance from `Random(seed)`, so the same kind, seed and shape give the same plant on every machine, and replaces every part, with what the mesh tools made of them. A tree is a tapered six-sided trunk up to where the crown begins (the height less the crown's radius), limbs leaving its upper half evenly around it, leaning 50 to 70 degrees, and crowns: the first on the trunk's top, the rest on the limbs' tips in turn (around the top without limbs). A bush is crowns on the ground, the first in the middle and as tall as the bush. `Grow` throws for a shape nothing grows from (`FoliageShape.IsValid`: 0.2 to 40 m tall, a crown of 0.1 to 15 m, a trunk up to 3 m wide, up to 12 limbs, 1 to 16 crowns).
- Shapes (`FoliageShapes`): `CreateTaper` is a trunk or limb standing on its origin along the up axis; `CreateBlob` is a crown, an icosahedron stretched to its radii, twenty flat faces, which keeps the hand-cut look and the mesh tools' work light.
- Placed once, a plant is an entity with the `foliage` component (`Plant`: the `file`). `FoliageSpawnSystem` gives Creations the kind: parts spawn nested under the entity in the editor and the game, each drawn by a `MeshRenderer` named `<file>#<part id>` (`FoliageMeshSource`). The trunk is a `StaticBody` whose `TriangleCollider` is its own triangles, so characters and paths go around it; limbs and crowns stop nobody.
- Scattered, it is an entity with the `foliageScatter` component (`FoliageScatter`: `file`, `count` 12, `radius` 4, `seed` 1, `minScale` 0.8, `maxScale` 1.2; at most `MaxCount`, 200, copies). `FoliagePlacement.Scatter` draws each copy's place from the seed, spread evenly over the disc, with a turn about the up axis and a size between the scales: the same every time. Every part of every copy is a part of the scatter, its id led by the copy's number (`3.crown.1`), drawing the file's own meshes, so copies share them. The copies stand flat at the entity's height (they do not follow the ground under them yet) and have no bodies.
- A change to the file respawns the parts of every plant and scatter naming it, keeping those whose ids stay.
- The creator's panel (`FoliagePanelViewModel` over `FoliageDocument`, a `CreationDocument<FoliageFile>`; `FoliagePanelView`): Type, Seed with New (another seed by chance), then the shape: Height, Crown, Leaves (the crowns' count) and, for a tree, Limbs and Trunk. Each choice grows the plant anew as one undo step in the file's history, held unsaved until saved, and reaches every placed copy. A number nothing grows from is put back.
- In the editor a foliage file dragged into the viewport is placed as one plant when it grows a tree and as a scatter when it grows a bush (`EntityPreset.ForFoliage`); either component can be added to any entity in the inspector. A double-click on a scatter makes its file alone, as its copies are not made in place.
- Not there yet: scatters following terrain or greyboxes under them, wind, leaf cards and looks from Blender, painting scatters with a brush, level of detail and instanced drawing (each part of each copy is its own entity, which the copy limit keeps in bounds).
