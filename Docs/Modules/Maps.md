# Maps

- Maps (the `Maps` module over Runtime, ported from FVNavigationSystem; `game.AddMaps(settings)`) know where things are. A `MapDefinition` (type `map`: priority, `AvailableWhen` conditions, layers) has `MapLayer`s (name, image, `Min` and `Max` corners on X and Z, optional height limits; `WorldToUv` and `UvToWorld`, with north at -Z and east at +X); `MapSystem` (a fixed system and service over every map of the content folder, `DataAssets.LoadAll`) finds the map and layer under the `navigator` (minimap radius, compass field of view, hidden marker categories), sending `ActiveMapChangedEvent`. Markers are `MarkerDefinition`s (type `marker`: icon, priority, `VisibleWhen`, fragments `minimap`, `compass`, `worldMap` and `discovery`), placed by a `mapMarker` component on an entity or by `AddMarker` at a point (`MarkerHandle`); visibility follows their conditions on every fact change, a `discovery` marker is discovered within its radius (its fact set, `OnDiscovered` applied), and one marker can be tracked and one waypoint placed (`MapSettings.WaypointMarker`). Views are plain data built on request: `BuildMinimapView` (markers within the radius around the navigator, turned against the view's heading, clamped to the edge when the marker says so), `BuildCompassView` (headings in degrees clockwise from north, within the field of view, with elevation above or below) and `BuildWorldMapView` (a layer's markers as uvs). The marker events (`MarkerAddedEvent`, `MarkerRemovedEvent`, `MarkerDiscoveredEvent`, `MarkerVisibilityChangedEvent`, `TrackedMarkerChangedEvent`, `WaypointChangedEvent`) tell the interface what changed.
